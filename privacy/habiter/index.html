Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how the Habiter application by Kukhol Apps handles information in connection with your use of the app. Habiter is a personal productivity and habit-tracking application designed to help users build better routines through consistent daily habits.

1. Information Habiter Stores

Habiter is designed primarily around on-device storage. The information described below is stored on your device as part of normal application operation:

  • habit information
  • habit categories and settings
  • completion history
  • streak information
  • XP and level progress
  • achievements
  • personalization preferences
  • reminder configuration

This information is stored on your device as part of normal application operation so that Habiter can display your habits, progress, and settings across app sessions.

2. Account and Cloud Storage

Habiter currently does not require a Habiter user account. Kukhol Apps currently does not operate cloud storage for your habit or progress information, and habit and progress information is not currently uploaded to a Kukhol Apps server.

This reflects the current architecture. Features and services may evolve over time, and any changes to data practices would be reflected in an updated privacy policy.

3. Information Habiter Does Not Currently Collect

Based on the current architecture of Habiter, the app does not currently collect through Kukhol Apps:

  • location
  • contacts
  • camera or photos
  • advertising identifiers
  • habit or progress information for advertising
  • active developer-operated analytics

This describes the current architecture and may change if Habiter's features or data practices change. Any such changes would be reflected in an updated policy.

4. Notifications and Reminders

Habiter may request notification-related permissions so it can show reminders you have selected. These permissions allow Habiter to schedule and display reminders for your habits at appropriate times.

You can grant or deny these permissions through your device settings. Habiter will respect your choices, and you can adjust reminder settings at any time within the app.

5. Third-Party and Platform Services

Certain optional or platform functionality in Habiter may interact with services supplied by the operating system or Google Play. Current integrated or platform-capable functionality includes:

  • Google Play In-App Review
  • Google Fonts runtime font retrieval and caching where applicable
  • Android or native sharing
  • Google Play Billing infrastructure, which exists in the application codebase

Google Play Billing infrastructure, which exists in the application codebase but is disabled for user purchases in this release. Users cannot initiate a purchase through the app, and Habiter does not currently sell subscriptions or paid products. The billing library remains in the app binary for a future release and may interact with Google Play services under Google's terms.

6. Data Sharing

Kukhol Apps does not currently operate a backend that receives your Habiter habit or progress data. Platform services that you invoke through Habiter, such as sharing or Google Play services, may process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Because features such as Google Fonts, Google Play services, and sharing can involve network or platform processing, this policy does not claim that no data can ever leave your device. What leaves the device is governed by the platform services you use and their respective policies.

7. Data Control and Deletion

Habiter provides a setting to reset the app's locally stored data. You can find it at:

Settings > Delete All Habiter Data

This allows you to reset Habiter's locally stored application data and scheduled reminders.

Uninstalling the application normally removes application-local data according to Android and platform behavior. This describes normal platform behavior and is not a guarantee beyond what the operating system provides.

8. Children's Privacy

Habiter is a general productivity application and is not specifically directed toward children. This policy does not assert any specific certification regarding children's privacy, nor does it create an arbitrary age restriction.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when Habiter's features, services, or data practices change. Updated versions will be made available through the app or the Kukhol Apps website.
