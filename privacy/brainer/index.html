Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how the Brainer application by Kukhol Apps handles information in connection with your use of the app. Brainer is an engaging cognitive training application designed to challenge memory, speed, logic, language, and attention skills.

1. Information Brainer Stores

Brainer is designed around an offline-first architecture. The information described below is stored locally on your device as part of normal application operation:

  • gameplay scores and challenge completion records
  • cognitive domain metrics (Memory, Speed, Logic, Language, Attention)
  • streak and daily mission progress
  • experience points (XP), brain levels, and brain ranks
  • unlocked milestone and hidden achievements
  • session history and AI-generated cognitive insights
  • application settings and visual customization preferences

This information is saved locally using structured device storage (Hive database) so that Brainer can display your progress and cognitive evolution between training sessions.

2. Account and Cloud Storage

Brainer does not require a user account to function. Kukhol Apps does not operate remote servers to store or collect your gameplay or cognitive progress data. All cognitive scores, achievements, and training records remain stored locally on your device.

3. Information Brainer Does Not Collect

Brainer does not collect through Kukhol Apps:

  • precise or coarse location data
  • contacts or address book entries
  • camera or photo gallery files
  • sensitive biometric measurement data
  • advertising identifiers or user tracking profiles

4. AI Insights and Analytics

Brainer analyzes your training sessions locally on your device to generate cognitive insights, performance breakdowns, and personalized daily recommendations. This analytics engine runs on-device and does not transmit your personal raw scores or performance details to external servers.

5. Third-Party and Platform Services

Certain optional or platform functionality in Brainer may interact with services provided by the operating system or Google Play:

  • Google Play In-App Review API for optional user feedback
  • Google Fonts runtime font rendering and caching where applicable
  • Native Android sharing features if invoked by the user

6. Data Sharing

Kukhol Apps does not sell, rent, or trade your personal or cognitive data. We do not operate a remote server that receives your application progress. Any platform interaction (such as system-level sharing or Google Play APIs) is governed by the operating system and respective third-party policies.

7. Data Control and Deletion

Brainer provides options to manage or clear your local training history inside the app:

Settings > Reset Training Timeline

You can also reset all application data at any time via your device settings (`Settings > Apps > Brainer > Clear Data`) or by uninstalling the application.

8. Children's Privacy

Brainer is a general cognitive fitness application designed for users of all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated as Brainer evolves. Any changes will be published on the Kukhol Apps website and reflected in this policy.
