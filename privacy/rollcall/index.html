Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how the RollCall application by Kukhol Apps handles information in connection with your use of the app. RollCall is a daily attendance-tracking application designed for school teachers to record classroom attendance.

1. Information RollCall Stores

RollCall is designed primarily around on-device storage. The information described below is stored on your device as part of normal application operation, entered directly by the teacher using the app:

  • class names and sections
  • student names and roll numbers
  • daily attendance records (present, absent, or late)
  • holiday and no-class day markings
  • reminder configuration

This information is stored on your device as part of normal application operation so that RollCall can display your classes, rosters, and attendance history across app sessions.

2. Account and Cloud Storage

RollCall currently does not require a RollCall user account. Kukhol Apps currently does not operate cloud storage for your class or attendance information, and this information is not currently uploaded to a Kukhol Apps server.

This reflects the current architecture. Features and services may evolve over time, and any changes to data practices would be reflected in an updated privacy policy.

3. Information RollCall Does Not Currently Collect

Based on the current architecture of RollCall, the app does not currently collect through Kukhol Apps:

  • location
  • contacts
  • camera or photos
  • advertising identifiers
  • class or attendance information for advertising
  • active developer-operated analytics

This describes the current architecture and may change if RollCall's features or data practices change. Any such changes would be reflected in an updated policy.

4. Notifications and Reminders

RollCall may request notification-related permissions so it can show a daily reminder to mark attendance. This permission allows RollCall to schedule and display a local reminder at the time you select.

You can grant or deny this permission through your device settings. RollCall will respect your choice, and you can adjust or disable the reminder at any time within the app's Settings.

5. Exporting and Sharing Data

RollCall allows a teacher to export a class's attendance as a CSV report, or export a full backup of the app's data as a file, for their own record-keeping and backup purposes. This export happens only when the teacher explicitly chooses to do so, using the device's own share or file functionality (for example, email, messaging apps, or cloud storage the teacher selects). Kukhol Apps does not receive, store, or have access to this exported data — it goes directly from the teacher's device to the destination the teacher chooses.

6. Third-Party and Platform Services

Certain optional or platform functionality in RollCall may interact with services supplied by the operating system or Google Play. Current integrated or platform-capable functionality includes:

  • Android or native sharing and file selection
  • Google Fonts runtime font retrieval and caching where applicable
  • Google Play In-App Review

7. Data Sharing

Kukhol Apps does not currently operate a backend that receives your RollCall class or attendance data. Platform services that you invoke through RollCall, such as sharing or file export, may process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Because features such as Google Fonts, Google Play services, and sharing can involve network or platform processing, this policy does not claim that no data can ever leave your device through those platform services. What leaves the device through RollCall's own features happens only when you explicitly export or share it.

8. Data Control and Deletion

RollCall allows a teacher to delete any class, which removes that class along with its students and attendance records from the device. RollCall also provides a settings section where a teacher can manage their data, including full backup and restore.

Uninstalling the application normally removes application-local data according to Android and platform behavior. This describes normal platform behavior and is not a guarantee beyond what the operating system provides.

9. Children's Privacy

RollCall is a productivity tool intended for use by teachers and school staff, not by children directly. While RollCall may store student names entered by a teacher for their own attendance record-keeping, RollCall is not directed at, marketed to, or designed for direct use by children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when RollCall's features, services, or data practices change. Updated versions will be made available through the app or the Kukhol Apps website.
