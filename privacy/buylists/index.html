Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how the BuyLists application by Kukhol Apps handles information in connection with your use of the app. BuyLists is a personal shopping-list, budgeting, and household organization application designed to help users plan and manage purchases.

1. Local-First Data Storage

BuyLists is designed around local-first storage. Your everyday application data is stored directly on your device rather than a central server. This includes:

  • Shopping lists and item entries
  • Saved lists and frequently used templates
  • Shopping history statistics
  • Pantry inventory items
  • Budget thresholds and spending limits
  • Application preferences (theme selection, accent color, and custom category colors)
  • Learned intelligence patterns and in-app insights

Storing this information locally ensures that the core features of BuyLists remain fast, offline-capable, and private.

2. User-Initiated List Sharing

BuyLists provides an optional feature allowing you to share lists. When you explicitly choose to share a shopping list, the list's contents (such as item names, quantities, units, prices, and categories) are uploaded to our secure cloud database (Google Firebase Firestore).

This upload generates a unique link containing a random document identifier. The shared list is stored in the database to allow recipients you share the link with to download and import the list into their own installation of BuyLists.

Only lists you explicitly choose to share are uploaded. Your personal budgets, pantry contents, and unshared list data remain exclusively on your device.

3. Diagnostics and Stability Services

To help us monitor app stability and fix technical errors, BuyLists integrates Google Firebase services (Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Core). When a crash or significant performance issue occurs, anonymous diagnostic telemetry (such as device model, operating system version, and technical crash logs) is sent to our developer account.

These stability diagnostics do not collect or transmit your shopping list contents, budgets, pantry items, or personal identity information.

4. Account Requirements

BuyLists does not currently require you to create a user profile, register an account, or log in to use the app. Core features remain fully accessible without account registration.

5. Notifications and Reminders

BuyLists may request permission to send you local push notifications for reminders, budget warnings, or streak notifications. These notifications are scheduled and processed locally on your device. No notification content or scheduling data is uploaded or processed on external servers.

You can enable or disable notifications at any time within your device's operating system settings.

6. Data Deletion and Control

You have full control over your locally stored data. BuyLists provides data management options in the Settings menu where you can clear your saved lists and shopping history, or reset learned intelligence data. Pantry inventory items can be managed and removed directly within the app's Pantry screen.

Uninstalling the application from your device normally removes all local application data, in accordance with operating system behavior. Shared lists uploaded to Firestore are maintained in our cloud-sharing database for retrieval by recipients.

7. Third-Party Services and Retours

Certain visual and platform capabilities in BuyLists utilize runtime platform retrievals, such as Google Fonts caching or native platform share interfaces. These actions conform to the privacy agreements of the respective operating system (Android/iOS) and Google Play services. BuyLists contains no commercial advertising SDKs or behavioral marketing trackers.

8. Children's Privacy

BuyLists is designed as a general organization tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiers from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our data practices or application functionality. Updated versions will be published on this website and highlighted in the application's documentation.
