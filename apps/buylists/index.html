BuyLists

Smarter shopping. Simpler organization.

BuyLists is a thoughtfully designed shopping-list and household organization app that helps you plan purchases, reuse lists, track spending, manage pantry items, and share shopping lists with family and friends.

Smart Shopping Lists

Create organized shopping lists with quantities, units, categories and prices.

Saved Lists

Save and reuse frequently needed shopping lists.

History & Spending

Review previous purchases and understand spending over time.

Pantry

Keep track of household items after completed shopping trips.

Flexible Budgets

Monitor shopping expenditure against optional daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly budget goals.

Smart Insights

Use shopping history and local intelligence features to surface helpful patterns and suggestions.

Share & Import

Share shopping lists with family or friends and allow recipients to import them into BuyLists.

Offline-First

Core shopping, saved-list, history and pantry functionality works locally on the device.

BuyLists is designed around local-first storage. Your everyday shopping, pantry and history data is primarily stored on your device. When you explicitly choose to share a list, the shared list data is uploaded to our cloud database to allow retrieval by recipients.
