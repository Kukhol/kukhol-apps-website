Attendance & Classroom Tools · RollCall

RollCall

Simple Daily Attendance

RollCall is a simple, offline-first attendance tracking application designed for school teachers, built to handle the real, everyday realities of classroom attendance — quickly, reliably, and without needing an internet connection or account.

  • Attendance
  • Offline-first
  • Teachers

Quick daily marking

Every student defaults to Present — mark only the exceptions, Absent or Late.

Roster import

Add students by pasting a list, one-tap clipboard paste, or importing a CSV file.

Backdated entries

Correct or fill in attendance for a day you missed, up to a year back.

Holiday marking

Mark a day as a holiday so it never shows as unmarked or pending.

Monthly summary

See each student's attendance percentage for the month, color-coded so at-risk attendance stands out.

Roster editing

Add, rename, or remove students any time — past attendance history is always preserved.

Daily reminder

A configurable local reminder so marking attendance never gets forgotten.

Backup and restore

Export a full backup of your data any time, and restore it whenever you need to.

RollCall's class, student, and attendance information is currently stored locally on the user's device and does not require a RollCall account.
