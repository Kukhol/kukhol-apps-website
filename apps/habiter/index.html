Habiter

Build Better Days

Habiter is a personal habit-tracking and productivity application designed to help you build better routines through consistent daily habits, meaningful progress and clear personal goals.

Habit tracking

Create and manage the habits that matter to you.

Daily completion

Mark habits complete each day and keep your routine moving.

Streaks

Build and maintain streaks that reflect your consistency.

XP and levels

Earn experience and level up as you stay consistent.

Achievements

Unlock achievements that recognize your progress.

Habit reminders

Schedule reminders to help you stay on track.

Personalization

Shape Habiter around your habits, categories and preferences.

Progress visibility

See your progress clearly so you know how far you've come.

Habiter's core habit and progress information is currently stored locally on the user's device and does not require a Habiter account.
